# Copado

> Copado is an AI-powered DevOps platform for Salesforce and other low-code SaaS clouds, providing CI/CD, release management, automated testing (Copado Robotic Testing), data deployment, and security/governance for enterprise Salesforce delivery. Backed by Insight Partners and SoftBank Vision Fund.
